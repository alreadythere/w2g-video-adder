W2G Video Adder

On w2g.tv: log in → Edit Profile → bottom of the page → New to generate a key. It's stored only in this browser profile (extension local storage). Treat it like a password.
Used when "Always use" is selected, or as a fallback when no room tab is open. The streamkey is the last part of a w2g.tv/rooms/… link.
Keyboard shortcuts (default Alt+Shift+Y add, Alt+Shift+U play now) can be changed at your browser's extension shortcut settings.
